How to use on a phone

1

Attach to your phone

Open the spring-loaded clamp and center it on the top edge of your phone (fits 67–90 mm widths), with the optical module opposite your camera.

2

Align with the camera

In the app, use the ±8 mm horizontal and ±5 mm vertical sliders to center the optical window over your lens, then lock the detent.

3

Position above the meat

Rest the scanner flat on the package. The built-in spacer ring keeps a fixed ~20 mm working distance and perpendicular angle.

4

Tap to scan

The 12 NIR LEDs illuminate the surface while the sensor captures the spectrum; data streams to your phone over BLE 5.3.

5

View your Trust Score™

The on-device AI model returns a 0–100 freshness score with a Low, Moderate, or High risk category, plus scan history.

Trust score™ risk categories

Low risk — fresher spectral profile
Moderate risk — proceed with caution
High risk — spoilage indicators present

The Trust Score is a confidence-based estimate from validated spectral data — a scientific assessment, not a guarantee of food safety.
